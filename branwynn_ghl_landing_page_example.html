Questions, answered

Good to Know Before You Begin

Every retreat is planned around the leader and their group. Start with a conversation about your vision and what may be possible.

Talk Through Your Idea
Who is this retreat planning service for?

It is designed for yoga and Pilates instructors, studio owners, coaches and other community leaders who want to host a group retreat.

How many people can join a retreat?

The SOW describes groups of approximately 6–20 travellers. The right group size depends on your retreat plan.

How far in advance should I plan?

Many retreat leaders plan 6–12 months ahead. Your timeline will depend on the destination, availability and your group's needs.

Do I need to organize everything myself?

The service is intended to help coordinate key elements such as venues, rooms, meals, transfers and itinerary planning.

What destinations are available?

Branwynn works across six regions. Share your preferred setting and the team can discuss options with you.
